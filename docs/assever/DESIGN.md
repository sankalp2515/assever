# Assever design direction

Purchase paperwork provides the structure: one approved purchase, a current obligation, and an observed receipt. These three records stay aligned as a purchase progresses.

Palette: harbor blue #143C68, clear blue #2057A0, ice #EDF4FA, white #FFFFFF, ink #183047, caution amber #825405. Blue denotes available action; words identify outcomes. Typography: Georgia for the one-word identity and main purchase heading, system humanist sans for controls and transaction detail. Amounts use tabular numerals.

Layout: a quiet left navigation and a broad work surface. The home view opens with a live, editable purchase specification rather than a generic metrics dashboard. On the work view, approved scope, obligations, and observed effects occupy ordered sections. Mobile stacks the same sections. Navigation is left aligned. Evidence rows expand inline.

Review: a paper-like cream/terracotta direction and decorative dashboard cards were rejected. The one memorable element is the purchase's large blue quantity equation. Everything else serves review, action or investigation. No external font or decorative image is required for offline operation.
